Qemal

Qemal is a RAG-based AI application built on our curated knowledge base and an Albania-tailored vector database. It delivers specialized, contextual knowledge about Albania, academic pathways, careers, and opportunities that generic AI tools simply cannot match. Powered by real expertise from our global mentor network, Qemal is built to be the most useful AI tool Albanian students have ever had.

Our team is actively building Qemal — launching in the coming months
